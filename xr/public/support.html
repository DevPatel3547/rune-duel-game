Rune Duel Support

Draw runes. Cast spells. Out-duel your rival.

How to play

  • Pick up to five spells for your spellbook on the main menu.
  • In a duel, draw a spell's rune with your finger (or mouse) in one stroke. Its card at the bottom of the screen shows the shape, and the dot marks where to start.
  • Cleaner and faster drawing makes a stronger spell. Every spell costs mana, which refills over time.
  • Drag along the bottom strip of the screen to sidestep incoming spells.
  • Shields block damage, but Pierce goes straight through them.

Playing online

  • Quick Match pairs you with another player. iPhone, iPad and web players all play together.
  • Create a room to get a six-letter code, and send it to a friend so they can join.
  • If your connection drops, you have about 15 seconds to reconnect and carry on the match.
  • If nobody is online, you can practise against the AI while you wait.

Something not working?

  • If the duel stutters, choose Low graphics on the main menu.
  • If online play will not connect, check your internet connection and update to the latest version.
