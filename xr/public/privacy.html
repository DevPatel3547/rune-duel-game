Privacy Policy

Rune Duel · Last updated 13 September 2026

Rune Duel is a spell-casting duel game for iPhone, iPad and the web. We built it to collect as little as possible. This page explains exactly what happens to your information.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.
  • No contacts, photos, location or other personal data from your device.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Online matches

When you play online, the game connects to our match server. It sends the display name you typed, your chosen spells, and your moves in the duel, which is what the server needs to run the match. This information is kept in the server's memory only while the match lasts. It is not saved to disk or a database, and it is gone when the match ends.

Like any internet service, our hosting provider (Fly.io) may briefly log connection details such as IP addresses to keep the service running and secure. We do not use them to identify you.

On your device

The game remembers a few settings on your own device, such as graphics quality. They never leave it.

Children

Rune Duel does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
